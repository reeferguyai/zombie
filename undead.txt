      The High and The UnDead

A Cannabis infused, end of the world, zombie, survival, crafting game similar in all ways to games like Live or Die, Last Day on Earth, Zgirls 2 Last One, Dawn of Zombies, No Way to Die, Prey Day, Lost in Blue, etc in those games you play as a human character against the zombies, in this game you play as a zombie character, well as a human character hat is just realizing that they are not human anymore..
