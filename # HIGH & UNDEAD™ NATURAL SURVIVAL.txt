# 2. GAME IDENTITY AND CENTRAL CONCEPT

HIGH & UNDEAD™ is an open-world survival game in which the player does not fight zombies as a human.

The player IS a zombie.

The protagonist is a newly infected female zombie who unexpectedly regains fragments of her human consciousness.

She remembers pieces of her former life but does not understand what happened, why she is different, or why she can think when other zombies cannot.

She must survive among two hostile populations:

- Humans who fear and hunt zombies.
- Zombies who instinctively attack anything that does not smell, move or behave like them.

The player must learn to blend into both populations.

The central gameplay experience revolves around:

**Survive. Gather. Craft. Hide. Adapt. Evolve. Remember.**

The game combines primitive survival, open-world exploration, stealth, crafting, zombie evolution, cannabis cultivation, blood mixing, animal companions, faction relationships and branching storytelling.

Create an original experience inspired by the broad gameplay concepts of survival sandbox games, zombie survival adventures and open-world crafting games. Do not copy copyrighted characters, assets, maps, music or storylines from existing games.

3. Wake up inside ChroniX.
4. Learn movement.
5. Explore the environment.
6. Encounter zombies.
7. Avoid human detection.
8. Avoid zombie detection.
9. Use a dumpster or barricade to hide for six seconds.
10. Collect a branch.
11. Collect a stone.
12. Craft the Multi-Stick.
13. Sharpen the Multi-Stick.
14. Use it to attack.
15. Use it to gather resources.
16. Find cannabis seeds.
17. Plant seeds in a dirt plot.
18. Watch the plant grow.
19. Harvest cannabis buds.
20. Smoke cannabis.
21. Reduce Bloodlust.
22. Find and collect blood.
23. Encounter a human survivor.
24. Encounter a faction-related event.
25. Exit the opening city district.
26. Save the game.
27. Reload the game and continue.
