# Implemented gameplay loop

Phoenix Blaze's Phaser prototype now connects these pieces into a playable vertical slice:

1. Explore a generated six-biome tile field with camera follow and collision obstacles.
2. Collect resources and track accepted quest objectives.
3. Fight infected that patrol waypoints, investigate, pursue, and attack.
4. Craft at the workbench, manage consumables and equipment, and make choices in faction conversations.
5. Record discoveries and story entries in the journal/map.
6. Save/load local browser slots, with periodic autosave.

Controls: WASD/arrows move, Space attack, E interact, I inventory, C craft at the workbench, V consume fictional Emberleaf, U weapon upgrade, J journal, M map, Escape pause.

The current art is generated pixel-style placeholder art; audio cues are synthesized with Web Audio. Replace these with licensed production assets as the game matures.
