# Phoenix Blaze - Game Design Document

## 1. Game Concept

**Title**: Phoenix Blaze: A Conscious Zombie Survival Game

**Core Premise**: Play as Phoenix Blaze, a newly-turned zombie who retains human consciousness. Manage your bloodlust with cannabis-based crafting while navigating a post-apocalyptic world where you must blend in with both humans and zombies.

## 2. Target Audience
- Age: 18+
- Interests: Survival games, sandbox gameplay, zombie narratives, mature content
- Platforms: Web browsers, eventually mobile

## 3. Unique Selling Points

1. **Play as the Zombie**: Rare perspective in zombie games
2. **Cannabis Mechanics**: Unique resource for survival and crafting
3. **Dual Stealth**: Blend in with both zombies AND humans
4. **Moral Choices**: Branching storylines based on decisions
5. **NSFW Content**: Mature relationships and customization
6. **AI-Driven NPCs**: Dynamic interactions with factions

## 4. Core Gameplay Loop

```
Explore → Gather Resources → Craft Items → Manage Bloodlust → Make Choices → Progress Story
```

## 5. Game Mechanics

### 5.1 Bloodlust System
- Meter increases over time (default: 0.1 per second)
- States: Calm (0-30%), Hungry (30-70%), Uncontrolled (70-100%)
- Cannabis consumption reduces meter
- Blood consumption reduces meter
- Effects on gameplay:
  - **Calm**: Normal stats, stealth bonus
  - **Hungry**: Increased damage, reduced stealth
  - **Uncontrolled**: Loss of player control, attacks randomly

### 5.2 Crafting System
- **Multi-Stick**: First tool (fight, dig, fish, chop)
- **Weapons**: Sticks, bows, spears, makeshift guns
- **Shelter**: Lean-tos, treehouses, rafts
- **Cannabis Items**: Suppressants, enhancers, potions
- **Clothing**: Disguises for blending in

### 5.3 Blending In
- **With Zombies**: Apply rotting meat/blood, mimic movements
- **With Humans**: Wear clean clothes, act normal, hide zombie features
- **Risk**: If disguise fails, both groups become hostile

### 5.4 Combat
- **Early**: Bite, scratch, brute strength
- **Mid**: Improvised weapons (rocks, sticks)
- **Late**: Crafted weapons (bows, spears, guns)
- **Bloodlust State**: Affects damage output and control

### 5.5 Blood Mixing
- **Animal Blood**: Fish, rat, rabbit, dog, cat, wolf, deer, bear
- **Human Blood**: Powerful but morally complex
- **Zombie Blood**: Unique effects
- **Combinations**: Different mixes grant different bonuses

## 6. World Design

### 6.1 Regions (6-8 total)
1. **Urban Ruins**: City streets, shops, skyscrapers
2. **Forested Suburbs**: Cabins, overgrown yards, camps
3. **Industrial Zone**: Warehouses, factories, tunnels
4. **Abandoned Lab**: Plot-relevant, scientific mysteries
5. **Swamp Lands**: Difficult terrain, rare resources
6. **Military Outpost**: High-risk, high-reward
7. **Beach Area**: Water-based exploration
8. **Mountain Setting**: Resources, hidden caves

### 6.2 Points of Interest
- Safehouses (save/rest/craft)
- Survivor camps (quests, trading)
- Abandoned buildings (loot, lore)
- High-risk zones (mutant zombies, toxic areas)

### 6.3 Day-Night Cycle
- **Day**: Humans more active, zombies passive
- **Night**: Zombies more aggressive, humans hiding
- **Weather**: Rain, fog, storms affect visibility and resources

## 7. Story & Narrative

### 7.1 Three Acts

**Act 1: Awareness and Survival**
- Phoenix wakes as a zombie, horrified
- Learn to suppress bloodlust
- Encounter survivors and other zombies
- Discover origins of outbreak

**Act 2: Understanding and Power**
- Investigate ChroniX Biotech
- Question humanity vs. zombie nature
- Gain new abilities
- Moral decisions affect relationships

**Act 3: Acceptance or Transformation**
- Final choice: Seek cure or embrace zombie nature
- Consequences based on player choices
- Multiple endings

### 7.2 Factions
- **The Resistance**: Anti-ChroniX survivors
- **The Scavengers**: Ruthless survivors
- **The Cultists**: Embracing zombie transformation
- **Lone Wolves**: Independent survivors

## 8. Character Customization

Players create Phoenix Blaze with options for:
- Face shape, body type, skin tone
- Breast size, hair (color/length/style)
- Tattoos, piercings, scars
- Clothing/nudity (NSFW options)
- Personality traits
- Backstory
