# Phoenix Blaze: A Conscious Zombie Survival Game

## Overview
**Phoenix Blaze** is an innovative HTML5-based open-world sandbox survival game where you play as a newly-turned zombie who retains human consciousness. Manage your bloodlust with cannabis-based crafting, blend in with both humans and zombies, and navigate a post-apocalyptic world of survival, moral choices, and self-discovery.

### Core Features
- 🧟 **Play as a Conscious Zombie**: Retain fragments of humanity while battling zombie instincts
- 🍃 **Cannabis Integration**: Craft marijuana-based items to suppress bloodlust and enhance abilities
- 🎭 **Stealth & Disguise**: Blend in with humans or mimic zombie behavior
- 🔨 **Deep Crafting System**: Create tools, weapons, shelters, and survival gear from gathered materials
- 🌍 **Open-World Exploration**: Semi-open world with 6-8 diverse regions
- 🤖 **AI-Driven NPCs**: Dynamic interactions with human survivors and other zombies
- 🎮 **Moral Decision-Making**: Branching storylines based on player choices
- 🔞 **Mature Content**: NSFW character customization and relationship mechanics

Phoenix Blaze
The Story of Ember Vale
Before The Fall

Long before she became known as Phoenix Blaze, she was simply Ember Vale.

Twenty-six years old.

A cannabis cultivator.

A botanist.

A dreamer.

She lived on the outskirts of a small mountain city, operating a legal craft cannabis farm known as Phoenix Grove, a place dedicated to medicinal genetics and sustainable cultivation.

Ember believed plants could heal almost anything.

Anxiety.

Trauma.

Addiction.

Pain.

She spent years crossbreeding unique strains designed to help veterans, cancer patients, and people suffering from severe PTSD.

Few people knew she had quietly become one of the top cannabis geneticists in the country.

Her greatest project was known as:

Project Phoenix

A strain unlike any before it.

Designed to increase neural repair.

Reduce aggression.

Promote emotional regulation.

Potentially slow neurodegenerative diseases.

Then the outbreak happened.

The Outbreak

Nobody knows where the infection started.

Some believe it came from:

A military lab
A pharmaceutical experiment
A fungal mutation
A biological weapon

Every region tells a different story.

Within weeks:

Governments collapsed
Cities burned
Communications failed
Humanity fractured

Most people infected lost everything.

Their memories.

Their identities.

Their souls.

But not Ember.

Day Zero

The attack happened while attempting to evacuate researchers from Phoenix Grove.

While helping survivors onto transport helicopters, a newly infected victim bit Ember on the neck.

The wound should have killed her.

Everyone thought it had.

For three days she drifted between life and death.

Then she woke up.

Hungry.

Terrified.

Changed.

But conscious.

Somehow Project Phoenix had altered her brain chemistry enough to preserve fragments of her humanity through the transformation.

She became something impossible.

A zombie who still remembered who she was.

The Birth Of Phoenix Blaze

The first weeks were horrific.

Every second she fought urges:

To hunt
To kill
To feed

Whenever bloodlust took over she would suffer flashes of memories.

Birthdays.

Family.

Laughter.

The smell of plants growing in greenhouses.

Each feeding stole another piece of herself.

But then she discovered something extraordinary.

Her own cannabis research slowed the urges.

Not permanently.

Not completely.

But enough.

Just enough.

For her to think.

To remember.

To survive.

The survivors who witnessed her refusing to attack gave her a nickname.

Phoenix Blaze

The woman who burned in death and rose again.

Neither living nor dead.

A bridge between worlds.
